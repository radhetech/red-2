Nuclear Medicine or Molecular Medicine is a modality in radiology imaging. It uses gamma radiating isotopes which are picked up by state of the art Gamma Cameras.

There are multiple procedures that can be performed in a nuclear medicine department. Here is a list showing the procedures and its uses in the diagnostic imaging.

  1. Neurology.
  2. Cardiology.
  3. Chest,Thorax,Abdomen.
  4. Skeletal.
  5. Lymphatics.
